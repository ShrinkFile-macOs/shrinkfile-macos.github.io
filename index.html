ShrinkFile for Mac

ShrinkFile is your all-in-one media toolkit. Compress, convert, and transcribe images, videos, and audio locally with pro-grade speed and total privacy. Built for professionals, developers, and anyone needing to free up storage—offline-first, every time.

Pro Video Compression

Shrink MOV, MP4, M4V, and MKV files while preserving original codecs like H.264, HEVC, VP9, and AV1. Smart bitrate control (Auto/Custom), adjustable resolutions up to 4K—no unnecessary transcoding.

Seamless Format Conversion

Batch convert MP4 ↔ MOV ↔ M4V ↔ WebM ↔ MKV ↔ AVI with lossless options, smart audio copying, and bitrate preservation. Extract audio to MP3, M4A, AAC, FLAC, WAV, or OGG effortlessly.

Powerful Image Tools

Smart compression for JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, AVIF, and GIF using MozJPEG and Zopfli. Handle animated images frame-by-frame. Batch convert formats and remove backgrounds with on-device AI.

Comprehensive Audio Suite

Compress MP3, M4A, FLAC, OGG, and WAV with full bitrate, sample rate, and channel control. Convert formats, trim visually with waveforms, transcribe audio-to-text, and generate speech.

Intelligent Workflow

Auto-preserves originals if compression doesn't shrink. Real-time ETA for tasks. Batch process multiple files. Everything runs locally for speed and privacy.
